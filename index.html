About Me

I am a Ph.D. student at the NLP Lab, Nanjing University, advised by Professor Jianbing Zhang. I received my M.S. from the same group and B.S. from the Kuang Yaming Honors School, Nanjing University. I also worked as a research intern at GenSI Lab in Institute for AI Industry Research (AIR), Tsinghua University, under the supervision of Professor Hao Zhou.

My research interests lie in AI for Science, particularly LLM for science, material representation learning and generative models. Previously, I worked on Vision–Language Models, emphasizing fine-grained cross-modal understanding and evaluation. I like various sports, such as basketball, table tennis, swimming.
